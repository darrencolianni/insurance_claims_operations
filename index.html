Insurance Claims Operations Analytics

Analyzing insurance claim workflows with Python, PostgreSQL, and Power BI

Project Overview

An end-to-end analytics project examining motor insurance claims operations using historical process event-log data. The analysis investigates claim processing times, workflow transitions, document requirements, and operational bottlenecks.

The dataset contains 32,058 process events across 5,131 claims, covering January 2014 through January 2020.

Technical Stack

  • Python: Data cleaning, exploratory analysis, and statistical testing.
  • PostgreSQL: Window functions, CTEs, process analysis, and reporting views.
  • Power BI: Interactive dashboard and operational reporting.
  • Docker: Reproducible development environment.

Key Findings

  • Glass damage and rodent bites account for 79.4% of claims.
  • Median claim lifecycle duration is 4.40 days, compared with a mean of 14.06 days.
  • 50.3% of claims exhibit repeated state patterns, a proxy for potential rework.
  • Document request to damage documents has a median transition time of 8.02 days.
  • Document volume is positively associated with claim duration (Spearman ρ = 0.349).
